# AgentOS Phase 5I — Live Customer Interview Capture System

**Version**: Phase 5I Interview Form v1.0  
**Directive**: This capture system provides a standardized, ready-to-use form for conducting and logging real 30-minute customer discovery interviews. **Zero customer responses, quotes, or commitments are fabricated.** Every field must be strictly classified using the 6-bucket data standard.  

---

## 1. Data Classification Standard

Every response captured during a customer interview must be explicitly tagged as one of:
1. `OBSERVED FACT`: Verifiable empirical truth stated by the customer.
2. `CUSTOMER QUOTE`: Direct verbatim statement captured during the interview.
3. `CUSTOMER OPINION`: Subjective projection or belief stated by the customer.
4. `OUR INFERENCE`: Logical deduction drawn by the AgentOS research team.
5. `OUR ASSUMPTION`: Unverified hypothesis held by the AgentOS team.
6. `UNKNOWN / NOT YET VALIDATED`: Parameter not discussed or unvalidated.
