# Wedge Evidence Scorecard V1

## Purpose

Use this scorecard after customer interviews to choose a first market based on evidence. Do not score a wedge until at least three relevant interviews have been captured.

## Scoring rules

- Score each criterion from 1 to 5.
- Write the evidence that supports every score.
- A score without a direct quote, observed fact, or concrete customer example is an assumption and should be marked unverified.
- A high average score cannot override a missing pilot path.

## Selection gate

A wedge can move to pilot specification only when all conditions are met:

1. At least three independent interviews identify comparable blocked actions.
2. At least one buyer describes a real cost, risk, or operational delay caused by the problem.
3. Existing tools have a named, evidence-backed limitation.
4. A limited-scope technical integration is feasible in a sandbox or staging environment.
5. At least one potential design partner agrees to discuss or test a pilot.
