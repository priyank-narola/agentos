# Discovery Evidence System V1

## Purpose

This is the operating record for Programme 1 in the Product Execution Master Plan. It converts customer conversations into a decision about the first product wedge. It is not a CRM, a sales pipeline, or a place to collect unnecessary personal data.

Use the candidate code in all working notes. Keep direct contact details only in the founder's chosen private contact tool. Do not record calls without explicit permission.

## Evidence rules

1. A direct quote, observed workflow, screenshot shared with permission, or verifiable operational fact is evidence.
2. A founder belief, an online comment, a vendor claim, or an AI summary is an assumption until a buyer confirms it.
3. One interview cannot choose the product. Record disagreement instead of averaging it away.
4. Never mark a candidate as a design partner unless they expressly agree to discuss or test a pilot.
5. No external message is sent from this document or automatically by the product process.

### Status vocabulary

`Not contacted` → `Invited` → `Scheduled` → `Completed` → `Synthesised`.

Use `Declined` or `Not a fit` when appropriate. Do not erase records; an unsuccessful route is useful evidence about buyer access.

## First-five synthesis format

Complete this after five interviews. It is a checkpoint, not a wedge choice unless the evidence is unusually decisive.

1. **What repeated?** List action families with interview count and direct evidence.
2. **What did not repeat?** Record attractive ideas that only appeared once.
3. **Where do current tools fail?** Separate native-tool gaps from adoption/training problems.
4. **Who feels pain and who pays?** Record roles, not guesses.
5. **What changed our assumptions?** State the original belief and new evidence.
6. **Decision:** Continue within a lane, rebalance remaining interviews, prepare a selection decision, or reject an assumption.

## Selection package after fifteen interviews

The selection package must contain all of the following before a vertical build begins:

- Completed interview records and first-five synthesis.
- Repeated-action matrix showing the exact action chosen.
- Completed `WEDGE_EVIDENCE_SCORECARD_V1.md` with evidence for every score.
- The buyer’s named alternative and specific unsolved control boundary.
- One feasible sandbox/staging connector path.
- At least one expressed design-partner discussion or pilot-review commitment.

If any item is missing, the output is “not ready to select,” not a forced product direction.
