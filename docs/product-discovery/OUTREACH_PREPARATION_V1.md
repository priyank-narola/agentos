# Outreach Preparation V1

## Purpose

Prepare for customer discovery without sending messages yet. Outreach begins only after the founder chooses the people and channels to contact.

## Priority interview profiles

| Market | First roles to seek | Best company type | What we need to learn |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Customer operations | Head of Support, VP Customer Experience, Support Operations lead | B2B SaaS, e-commerce, marketplace using AI support tools | Which customer action is too risky for an AI agent to complete alone |
| Finance operations | Controller, Head of Finance Operations, Treasury lead, Accounts Payable lead | Fintech, marketplace, SaaS company with payments or automated invoices | Which money-related action needs a human and why existing workflow tools are insufficient |
| IT and cloud operations | CTO, Platform Engineering lead, DevOps lead, SRE lead | 50 to 500 person software company using coding or operations agents | Which production action an agent cannot safely execute through current review and deployment controls |

## Recruitment sources

- Founder and personal network introductions.
- Former classmates, colleagues, mentors, and alumni working in relevant teams.
- Local startup communities and technical meetups.
- LinkedIn searches for the target roles and company types.
- Product, engineering, finance, and support communities where AI-agent adoption is already discussed.

## Outreach principles

- Ask for a short research conversation, not a purchase.
- Do not present a product claim or promise features.
- Personalise the message using a real reason for contacting the person.
- Keep the request to 20 minutes.
- Do not claim benchmarks, customers, performance, or production readiness that have not been independently verified.

## Draft research invitation

> Hi [First name], I am researching how teams decide which customer, finance, or production actions an AI agent can safely perform. I am not selling anything in this conversation. I want to understand one real workflow where your team still needs human approval or avoids automation because the consequence of a mistake is too high. Would you be open to a 20-minute research conversation? I will keep the discussion confidential and can share the aggregated findings afterward.

## Personalisation prompts

### Customer operations

Mention that the company uses an AI support tool, has a high-volume support operation, or operates subscriptions, orders, payments, or refunds.

### Finance operations

Mention that the company handles payouts, payments, invoices, vendor workflows, or a regulated finance process.

### IT and cloud operations

Mention that the company is actively investing in engineering automation, cloud operations, developer tooling, or AI-assisted development.

## Tracking fields

For every potential interview, record only what is necessary:

| Field | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Candidate code | CUSTOPS-01 |
| Market | Customer operations |
| Role | Head of Support |
| Company type | B2B SaaS |
| Source | Founder introduction or public professional profile |
| Outreach status | Not contacted, invited, scheduled, completed, declined |
| Notes | Why this profile is relevant |

Do not collect unnecessary personal information. Do not log private conversation details outside the evidence record without the interviewee's consent.

## Ready-to-send gate

Before external outreach begins, confirm:

1. The founder approves the chosen contact channels and message tone.
2. The interview guide is final enough for a first conversation.
3. The evidence tracker is ready.
4. Each message is truthful and personalised.
