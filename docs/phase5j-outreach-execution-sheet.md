## 1. Allowed Status Definitions

- `NOT SENT`: Outreach prepared, awaiting manual dispatch by Priyank.
- `SENT`: Message manually dispatched by Priyank.
- `DELIVERED`: Message confirmed delivered to public contact channel or InMail inbox.
- `REPLIED`: Customer has sent a direct written response.
- `SCHEDULED`: 30-minute discovery interview confirmed on calendar.
- `COMPLETED`: Discovery interview finished and raw notes captured.
- `NO RESPONSE`: No response received after initial message and follow-ups.
- `DECLINED`: Target explicitly declined to participate in research.
- `CONTACT NOT VERIFIED`: Specific individual contact details not publicly available.
